01

Technology

We decompose intelligence into intelligence atoms and reactor networks. Intelligence atoms store knowledge, skills, and input information. Reactor networks take relevant intelligence atoms as inputs and apply them to perform intelligent work and produce outputs. Starting from either model training data or a finished model, we build large stores of atoms, then train reactors that are starved of representational power, which forces them to operate on atoms to produce outputs. Reactor networks are on the order of hundreds of millions of parameters, making them far smaller than today's LLMs. Yet, when paired with atoms, they perform on par with or better than the LLMs.

The results of atomizing intelligence are Atomized Intelligence Containers (AICOs) and Atomized Intelligence Caches (AICAs). AICOs pair compact reactor networks with large collections of intelligence atoms prepared in advance. AICAs begin as AICOs, then serve requests for intelligence or intelligent actions locally; when needed, they delegate to cloud frontier models through APIs, analyze the responses, and use them to enrich the atoms in the AICA by adding new atoms or modifying existing ones. This enables hundreds of generated tokens per second on inexpensive CPUs, without any specialized AI accelerator. AICOs and AICAs can run on smartphones and laptops, driving the marginal cost of frontier intelligence toward zero.

02

Key Advantages

  1. 01

    Extremely low cost at superhuman thinking speeds. Hundred-million-parameter reactor networks use architectures built for fast inference with intelligence atoms, producing hundreds of tokens per second on commodity CPUs.

  2. 02

    Local, private deployability. AICOs and AICAs can be deployed directly on users’ devices, keeping sensitive data and intelligence workflows private.

  3. 03

    Full interpretability. Knowledge resides in explicit intelligence atoms: inspect, understand, or edit the knowledge base directly.

  4. 04

    Continual learnability. A single sample can be analyzed (batch size = 1) to enrich the atom store, with the result available on the very next inference.

  5. 05

    Safety by inspection. Prescribed behavior is visible in the intelligence atoms; no behavior or knowledge is inherent to the reactor networks themselves.

05

Open Research

Total Safety

We will achieve 99.99% to 100% safety and reliability by inspecting every atom and studying how it interacts with other atoms. Reactors have no agency and are starved of long-term memory beyond basic application rules, so any pathological behavior must reside in the atoms.

Federated Atomized Intelligence

Each user will enrich their own AICO or AICA with different knowledge. Those changes will be exported and merged so everyone benefits, while contradictions and conflicts will be surfaced for people to resolve. This federated approach will ultimately replace model pretraining.

Atomizing Robotic Intelligence

Robotic intelligence (control, perception, and decision-making) will also be seamlessly atomized because most current solutions are vision-language-action models. Decomposition into reusable atoms will make robotic systems faster and cheaper to run, easier to inspect, and able to learn continually without retraining an entire model.
